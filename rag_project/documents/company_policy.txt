Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager approval. Remote workdays must be logged in the HR system by the end of each week. Employees are expected to be reachable during core hours, from 10 AM to 4 PM local time, regardless of their work location.

Equipment Policy

The company provides a laptop and a monitor for all remote employees. Additional equipment requests must be submitted through the IT portal and approved by a team lead. Employees are responsible for keeping company equipment secure and up to date.
